* Settings → Pictures and videos: one place to add tile pictures, backdrop pictures and videos, each kept once.
* Apps and folders can have their own backdrops (Edit → Backdrops): one always shows, more take turns.
* Folders can have a tile picture; one picture can go on several tiles.
* Custom wallpapers has its own list.
* In a picking grid, OK ticks or unticks; hold OK to delete.
* A folder's Rename is now under Edit, as an app's is.
